Choosing between them often depends on whether a managed runtime is acceptable for the target domain.

This critique highlights a growing problem in open-source maintenance where AI-generated 'slop' issues waste maintainers' time and degrade project health, potentially affecting developer productivity and the sustainability of open-source projects. Ronacher specifically calls out that AI-generated reports often include fake-minimal reproductions, incorrect root cause guesses, and irrelevant error lists, and he proposes a four-point format: command run, expected behavior, actual behavior, and exact error/log.
