## [Armin Ronacher Slams AI-Generated Bug Reports](https://simonwillison.net/2026/May/24/armin-ronacher/#atom-everything) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Armin Ronacher, creator of Flask and Jinja, criticized AI-generated bug reports for being verbose, inaccurate, and full of confident guesswork, advocating for a simple three-step format based on human observation. This matters because AI-generated content is degrading the quality of bug reports in open source projects, wasting maintainers' time and undermining trust in issue tracking. Ronacher's proposed format offers a clear, actionable standard for improving developer experience. Ronacher's proposed format asks reporters to state: (1) the command run, (2) expected behavior, (3) actual behavior, and (4) exact error or log. He notes that AI-generated reports often contain fake minimal reproductions, wrong root cause guesses, and irrelevant error lists.

rss · Simon Willison · May 24, 18:46

**Background**: Bug reports are essential for open source maintenance, but AI tools like ChatGPT are increasingly used to generate them. These reports often lack the reporter's own voice and include hallucinated details, making them harder to triage. Ronacher's post reflects a growing frustration among maintainers with AI-generated 'slop' in issue trackers.

## [DeepSeek Reasonix: Low-Cost Coding Agent with High Caching](https://esengine.github.io/DeepSeek-Reasonix/) ⭐️ 7.0/10

DeepSeek Reasonix is a terminal-based AI coding agent designed specifically for DeepSeek's API, achieving over 90% prefix cache hit rate through append-only conversation management, reducing long-session costs to about one-fifth of normal. This tool demonstrates how cache-first design can dramatically lower the cost of AI-assisted coding, making it accessible for longer sessions and more frequent use, which could shift developer workflows toward more affordable AI integration. Reasonix uses a 'cache-first' loop and 'Flash-first' cost control, directly connecting to api.deepseek.com without a protocol translation layer, and automatically fixes tool calls to maintain cache stability.

hackernews · Alifatisk · May 24, 13:02 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48256953)

**Background**: Prefix caching in LLM APIs allows reusing previously computed key-value (KV) cache when the beginning of a prompt matches earlier requests, significantly reducing latency and cost. DeepSeek offers a discounted cache hit rate, making cache optimization especially valuable. Other tools like Claude Code have reported cache hit rates around 92% with 81% cost reduction.
